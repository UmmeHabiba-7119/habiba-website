## Journal Articles

### Fresh Fish Disease Detection in Aquaculture: Leveraging CNN Models for Accurate Diagnosis

**Authors:** Umme Habiba, et al.  

**Publication Type:** Journal Article  

Aquatic products, especially freshwater fish, play an important role in meeting global protein demand. Increasing fish production is therefore essential. However, the aquaculture industry faces significant challenges due to various fish diseases. This research applies convolutional neural network models to support accurate and efficient fish disease diagnosis.
