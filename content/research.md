My research focuses on the application of Artificial Intelligence to real-world challenges. I am particularly interested in developing data-driven and intelligent systems for agriculture, environmental monitoring, computer vision, and sustainable development.

---

## Research Focus

### Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning

I am interested in designing machine learning solutions that can analyze complex data, identify meaningful patterns, and support accurate decision-making in practical applications.

### Deep Learning and Computer Vision

My work explores deep learning techniques for image and video analysis, including object detection, classification, tracking, behavior analysis, and visual monitoring.

### Remote Sensing and Environmental Analysis

I am interested in using satellite imagery, geospatial data, and machine learning to study environmental change, vegetation dynamics, land surface temperature, and long-term ecological patterns.

### AI for Agriculture and Livestock

Another important area of my research is the use of computer vision and intelligent monitoring systems in agriculture and livestock management. This includes automated observation, health assessment, behavior analysis, and decision-support applications.

---

## Research Approach

My research follows an interdisciplinary and application-oriented approach. I aim to combine:

- Artificial intelligence and machine learning
- Image and video processing
- Remote sensing and geospatial analysis
- Data-driven prediction and monitoring
- Real-world problem solving

The goal is to develop solutions that are technically sound, practically useful, and socially relevant.

## Research Vision

My long-term research vision is to develop intelligent technologies that contribute to sustainable agriculture, environmental protection, healthcare, and societal well-being. I am especially interested in interdisciplinary collaborations that connect computing research with practical challenges.
